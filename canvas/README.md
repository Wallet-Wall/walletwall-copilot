# WalletWall canvas

The implementation lives inside the plugin, because a Copilot plugin ships its canvas with it: `plugin/com.github.copilot/extensions/walletwall-evidence-explorer/`. The folder and the canvas id keep their historical name to avoid churn; the canvas is presented to users as **WalletWall**. This directory only holds the contract the implementation must keep.

## What it shows (one evidence bundle)

**The Wall** (the centre of the panel), drawn as a Coinstellation: every source, authority, observation, claim, WalletWall derivation and the vault readiness keystone of the bundle as nodes of one deterministic graph, with the claims' subjects (chain, contract, wallet, stablecoin) beside the claims they are about. Each claim node carries exactly the support state the MCP server returned. Beside it, for the piece in focus: its state and at most six facts (support, freshness, finality, authority, conflicts, the limitation count), with Provenance (the `trace_provenance` lineage), Details (subject and value as presented, authority class, corroboration, supersession, policy), Limitations (each code with a plain-language description) and **Sources** one step away. **Trace** emphasises the selected claim's provenance on the same graph. Below: the **Wallprint** band, the **Readiness assembly**, a searchable list of every piece, the criteria table and "does not establish" list, a legend, a reserved **Receipt** row and a text version of The Wall. On a phone the same content is composed as a focused instrument: a header with the state strip, The Wall as the stage in a tall layout, the detail as a bottom sheet (peek, half, full); nothing is dropped and nothing is computed on the page. Design rules: `docs/CANVAS_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

## Contract

1. It renders structured results of the existing MCP tools; it does not compute states and does not add evidence. Every state comes from a call to the bundled `walletwall-mcp.mjs` made by the canvas itself, so the panel cannot drift from what the tools return. A state value outside the WWEP enumerations is displayed as "not recognised", never as a state. The Wall's layout is geometry only: it never ranks, scores or resolves evidence.
2. Same privacy posture: local only, no outbound network, no persistence, no telemetry. The one listener is loopback-only, which the Copilot app requires for canvases. Fonts (Sora, Manrope, IBM Plex Mono) and brand images (the WalletWall brick-wallet mark, WalletWallaby) are embedded as `data:` URIs; the page's CSP allows `data:` for fonts and images and nothing else beyond its own origin. See `docs/PLUGIN_PATH_PRIVACY.md`.
3. Read-only. No action signs, sends, swaps or writes.
4. No colour or wording that implies safety or approval: no green "verified" badge; `ESTABLISHED` is described as "presented evidence satisfies the published policy"; terracotta marks focus and attention (the piece in focus and its path, the Wallmark, conflict and staleness), never good or bad; every state is carried by ring, line, fill and label, not colour alone.
5. Agent-callable actions: `set_bundle`, `select_assertion`, `get_view`. `get_view` returns the MCP results the panel shows, so the agent describes the panel without re-deriving anything.
6. **Addresses** are printed in full in every detail, list and rail, exactly as the bundle presents them (no shortening, no case or checksum change), as selectable text, with the chain ID next to them and two controls: Copy (exactly the full value) and, when the chain is stated, Copy chain + address (`chainId:<id> address:<full value>`). A piece on The Wall shows a short label (Contract, Wallet) and never an address; its accessible name, tooltip and detail carry the full value. Nothing is stored because an address was copied, and the status line does not repeat the address.
7. The listener has four routes (`GET /`, `GET /view`, `GET /events`, `POST /select`), requires an unguessable path key and the exact `Host`, and refuses a foreign `Origin` or a non-JSON body on `POST /select`.
8. **Only the Observed lens exists.** Compare, Replay, Scenario, Receipt (Copy receipt, Verify) and QR are shown as disabled, reserved affordances with their reason on screen ("Requires immutable snapshot"; "Scenario — simulation engine not yet enabled"). Nothing generates a receipt, a QR code or a snapshot.
9. The pieces settle once when the bundle changes and do not drift afterwards. Restrained loops (the conflict ring, the selection halo, stale recession, the traced path, the guide's idle) animate transform and opacity only, accompany states that are also drawn statically, and stop under reduced motion (system setting or the panel's own toggle). The WalletWallaby guide is optional and can be hidden; it is never needed to operate the panel.
10. **One selection, shared.** The selected assertion is the explorer's `selectedId`: a click on a claim sets it through `POST /select`, exactly as the agent's `select_assertion` does, and the page re-renders from the explorer's view. A click on any other piece is local visual focus only; it never becomes an assertion selection or a provenance request. The Wall is a projection of the explorer's view (`coinstellation-model.mjs`): it creates, strengthens or replaces no evidence state.

## Opening it

`open_canvas` with `canvasId: "walletwall-evidence-explorer"` and input `{ "fixture": "usdc-established" | "stale-identity" | "conflicting-balances" | "partial-provider-balance" | "unknown-balance" | "superseded-balance" | "readiness-blocked" }` for a bundled synthetic example, or `{ "bundle": <WWEP bundle>, "evaluatedAt": "<UTC time>" }` for the same input you pass to `inspect_evidence`.

## Deferred

Everything beyond one bundle and the Observed lens: immutable snapshots, receipts, QR verification, replay of historical states, scenarios or simulation, multi-bundle comparison, diffing, export, editing evidence. See `docs/DESKTOP_INSTALL_PROOF.md` for what has and has not been verified inside the Desktop app.
